# Vectorae help center

Every article describes what the product does, checked against its code, with the date it was last verified.

Not live yet: LinkedIn steps; Stripe checkout (paying for a plan online). Where this article describes them, they are built and tested only against simulated services. Free trials and online plan changes described here start through online checkout, which is not live yet; to start, [book a call](https://www.calendarharvest.com/contact/). Features marked off by default must be switched on by an administrator. See [known limitations](https://www.calendarharvest.com/help/known-limitations/).

## All articles

- [What is Vectorae?](https://www.calendarharvest.com/help/what-is-leadpilot/): Vectorae is an outbound sales system.
- [Who Vectorae is for, and who it is not for](https://www.calendarharvest.com/help/who-its-for-and-not-for/): This article helps you decide whether Vectorae fits your business before you sign up.
- [Getting started with Vectorae](https://www.calendarharvest.com/help/getting-started/): This article walks you through your first session: creating an account, verifying your email and phone, choosing a plan, and launching your first strategy.
- [The research pipeline](https://www.calendarharvest.com/help/research-pipeline/): When you launch a strategy, Vectorae runs a research pipeline: a fixed series of AI research steps that ends in a written strategy document.
- [Strategy verification (the 10 checks)](https://www.calendarharvest.com/help/strategy-verification/): After the research pipeline finishes, Vectorae checks the strategy document against 10 separate criteria before anything can reach a real person.
- [Lead sourcing and email verification](https://www.calendarharvest.com/help/lead-sourcing-and-email-verification/): Once a strategy is verified, Vectorae can find people who match it, look up their email addresses and check that those addresses accept mail.
- [Personalization and claim verification](https://www.calendarharvest.com/help/personalization-and-claim-verification/): Vectorae writes each outreach message for one person, using what it actually knows about them.
- [Sequences and follow-ups](https://www.calendarharvest.com/help/sequences-and-follow-ups/): A sequence is the series of messages Vectorae sends to each lead in a strategy.
- [Sending email through Gmail](https://www.calendarharvest.com/help/email-sending-gmail/): Vectorae sends outreach email from the Gmail account you connect, automatically, inside a send window and a daily limit that grows slowly while the account warms up.
- [WhatsApp outreach](https://www.calendarharvest.com/help/whatsapp-outreach/): Vectorae can send outreach on WhatsApp through Meta's WhatsApp Business Cloud API.
- [LinkedIn outreach and your LinkedIn network](https://www.calendarharvest.com/help/linkedin/): Vectorae has two separate LinkedIn features.
- [The send-review queue and approvals](https://www.calendarharvest.com/help/send-review-queue/): Vectorae sends outreach automatically.
- [Replies and the unified inbox](https://www.calendarharvest.com/help/replies-and-unified-inbox/): When a prospect replies by email, WhatsApp or LinkedIn, Vectorae stores the reply, classifies it, and changes what happens next in their sequence.
- [Booking meetings and your calendar](https://www.calendarharvest.com/help/booking-and-calendar/): Prospects can book a meeting with you in two ways: through a booking page on Vectorae's own calendar, or through a Calendly link.
- [Meetings, prep briefs, the meeting room and transcripts](https://www.calendarharvest.com/help/meetings-prep-room-transcripts/): Once a prospect books, Vectorae helps you prepare for the call, run it, and follow up.
- [CRM and integrations](https://www.calendarharvest.com/help/crm-and-integrations/): Vectorae has its own CRM, and it can also connect to HubSpot, Salesforce, Slack, and tools like Zapier or Make.
- [The learning loop and A/B tests](https://www.calendarharvest.com/help/learning-loop-and-ab-tests/): Vectorae records what happens to every message it sends (sent, replied, booked and so on) and uses those records to inform future work.
- [Analytics, health score and ROI](https://www.calendarharvest.com/help/analytics-health-score-roi/): Vectorae reports on your campaigns in several places: the Analytics page, the Revenue & ROI page, a shareable ROI dashboard, and a Pipeline Health Score.
- [Deliverability and mailbox health](https://www.calendarharvest.com/help/deliverability-and-mailbox-health/): Deliverability is whether your emails reach the inbox.
- [Compliance (CAN-SPAM, GDPR, CASL, PDPA)](https://www.calendarharvest.com/help/compliance/): Vectorae builds several compliance safeguards into how it sends: an unsubscribe route in every email, a suppression list checked right before every send, rules that depend on where the recipient is, and a record of what was checked.
- [Privacy, data retention and deletion](https://www.calendarharvest.com/help/privacy-data-retention-deletion/): This article explains what Vectorae stores about you and about the prospects you contact, how long it keeps it, and what you can download or delete yourself.
- [Plans, limits and billing](https://www.calendarharvest.com/help/plans-limits-billing/): Every Vectorae account is on a plan.
- [Account security, sign-in and API keys](https://www.calendarharvest.com/help/account-security/): This article covers how signing in works, how long you stay signed in, how to change or reset your password, how personal API keys work, and why an account can be blocked or suspended.
- [Workspaces, team roles and agency mode](https://www.calendarharvest.com/help/workspaces-and-agency-mode/): A workspace lets other people work on your Vectorae account with a role that limits what they can do.
- [Pausing and stopping outreach](https://www.calendarharvest.com/help/pause-stop-kill-switch/): Vectorae sends outreach automatically from a server, so closing the app does not stop it.
- [The Android app](https://www.calendarharvest.com/help/mobile-app/): Vectorae has an Android app.
- [Troubleshooting](https://www.calendarharvest.com/help/troubleshooting/): This article covers the problems customers run into most: a strategy that seems stuck, a strategy that failed verification, Gmail that needs reconnecting, error codes such as 402, 429 and 503, outreach that is not sending, and bounces.
- [Glossary of Vectorae terms](https://www.calendarharvest.com/help/glossary/): This page explains the words Vectorae uses in the app and in this help center.
- [Known limitations](https://www.calendarharvest.com/help/known-limitations/): This is an honest list of what Vectorae does not do, does not do yet, or has not yet proven against the real outside services it connects to.
- [Contacting support and checking service status](https://www.calendarharvest.com/help/contact-support-and-status/): You can get help inside Vectorae in two ways: ask the support assistant, or send a ticket to the team.
- [Running Vectorae on your own: a solo founder's routine](https://www.calendarharvest.com/help/guide-solo-founder/): This guide is for one person running Vectorae alone: one product, one strategy, and a short list of screens to check each day and each week.
- [Working as an SDR in someone else's workspace](https://www.calendarharvest.com/help/guide-sdr-team-member/): This guide is for someone invited into another person's Vectorae workspace with the SDR role (and, briefly, the viewer role).
- [Your product profile and ideal customer profile](https://www.calendarharvest.com/help/product-profile-and-icp/): Everything Vectorae researches, every lead it finds and every score it gives starts from what you type into the New strategy form.
- [Strategy patterns and playbooks](https://www.calendarharvest.com/help/strategy-patterns-and-playbooks/): Vectorae groups strategies that aim at the same kind of buyer with the same kind of outreach, and keeps a running score of how that combination performs.
- [Google Workspace DNS setup (SPF, DKIM, DMARC) and Gmail access](https://www.calendarharvest.com/help/google-workspace-dns-setup/): If you send from a Gmail address on your own Google Workspace domain, Vectorae checks that domain's SPF, DKIM and DMARC records.
- [HubSpot and Salesforce sync in detail](https://www.calendarharvest.com/help/crm-sync-field-mapping/): This article lists exactly which fields Vectorae writes to HubSpot and Salesforce, which changes it reads back, how often, and what happens when a record changes on both sides.
- [Webhook events, signatures and Slack alerts](https://www.calendarharvest.com/help/webhooks-and-slack-events/): This is the technical reference for Vectorae's outbound webhooks and its Slack alerts: every event you can subscribe to, what each delivery contains, how to check its signature, how retries work, and which events reach Slack.
- [Reply classification and Reply Copilot](https://www.calendarharvest.com/help/reply-copilot-and-classification/): This article explains how Vectorae decides what kind of reply it received, which consequences follow automatically and which are only suggestions, and how Reply Copilot drafts answers for you to approve.
- [Daily send approval and notifications](https://www.calendarharvest.com/help/daily-send-approval-and-notifications/): Before Vectorae sends anything on its own on a given day, a person has to say yes for that channel.
- [Annual billing and changing your plan](https://www.calendarharvest.com/help/annual-billing-and-plan-changes/): This article explains the yearly way to pay for Vectorae, what changes when you move to a higher or lower plan, and what happens to money you have already paid and to campaigns that are already running.
- [Benchmarks and what good looks like](https://www.calendarharvest.com/help/benchmarks-what-good-looks-like/): This article collects the numbers Vectorae itself uses to judge a campaign: the reply, bounce and meeting figures it compares or acts on, and the score bands of its health scores.
- [Security FAQ for buyers](https://www.calendarharvest.com/help/security-faq-for-buyers/): This article answers the security questions a buyer or security reviewer usually asks before choosing Vectorae: how passwords, keys and secrets are stored, how webhooks are signed and kept away from internal systems, what audit records exist, and where the service runs.
- [Data processing and subprocessors](https://www.calendarharvest.com/help/data-processing-and-subprocessors/): This article lists the outside companies that store or process data for Vectorae, what each one receives, and where the data is hosted.
