For businesses that sell to other businesses

Vectorae is an AI outbound sales tool for agencies and B2B service firms: it researches your market, finds and verifies business contacts, writes one-to-one emails, sends on a schedule you approve each day, and books meetings.

  • Agencies

    Marketing, web and design agencies where the founder still does the selling and the pipeline stalls whenever client work gets busy.

  • B2B service firms

    Staffing and recruiting agencies, consultancies and fractional experts who sell to other businesses and have no sales team yet.

  • Local businesses with business clients

    Commercial cleaning, facilities, HVAC and fire protection companies looking for property managers, offices and other business accounts nearby.

Not a fit if you are

  • Selling to consumers or homeowners. Vectorae only finds people at companies.
  • Emailing a list you already have. Leads come from Vectorae's own sourcing; you cannot import them.
  • High-volume sending. By default each mailbox sends at most 100 emails a day.

Compliance is part of the send path

  • Unsubscribe in every email

    Every email has a footer with your business identity and an unsubscribe link, plus one-click unsubscribe headers. It takes effect immediately, with no login.

  • Suppression on every channel

    An unsubscribe or a "stop" reply suppresses the person on email, LinkedIn, WhatsApp and phone. The list is checked when leads are sourced and again right before every send.

  • Daily send approval

    On by default. Automatic sending waits each day until the account owner approves it. LinkedIn steps, once live, need their own approval.

  • GDPR deletion and export

    Erase a prospect from their lead page, download a copy of your data, and delete your own account from your profile.

These are safeguards in the software, not legal advice. You remain responsible for having a lawful basis to contact each person. Privacy Policy

Questions

More in the full walkthrough or ask us.

Do I have to send the messages myself?

No. Once you launch a sequence and approve the day's sending, Vectorae sends automatically. Before every send it re-checks the suppression list, the campaign and contact state, the send window and the daily allowance.

Will Vectorae reply to prospects for me?

No. Replies are classified and routed to you, with a suggested next step and a draft you can edit. You write and send every answer.

Can I upload my own contact list?

Not as leads. Leads come from Vectorae's own sourcing, which checks every email address. On Growth plans and up you can upload your LinkedIn connections export; messaging those connections uses LinkedIn steps, which are not live yet.

How many emails does it send a day?

By default, a newly connected Gmail account starts at 10 emails on its first day, adds 10 a day, and stops at 100 a day. Messages over the day's allowance wait for the next day; they are not dropped.

Does it work outside the United States?

It works out each lead's region and applies that region's rules: for example, no open-tracking pixel for leads in the EU, EEA or UK, and a regional notice in the footer where needed. You are still responsible for the outreach rules that apply to you.

Is there a mobile app?

There is an Android app built from the same code as the web app, with the same sign-in. There is no iPhone app. Contact us for the current way to install it.

What is not live yet?

LinkedIn steps and Stripe checkout (paying for a plan online): built and tested only against simulated services, not yet live. Several other features are built but switched off by default; they are listed above as available on request.
